## Rules

Last Brick is a turn-based construction game for two to six players on one device, or one player against the computer. Finish buildings to win stars. The player with the most stars after six rounds wins. Tied players share their rank.

### On your turn

1. Click or tap how many bricks to place, then choose one building on the map or in **Building sites**.
2. If you place the last needed brick, you earn all of that building’s stars. If you picked more bricks than the site needs, you spend only the bricks that fit.

Each player has 12 bricks for the game. The default opening limit is 2 bricks per player, and later turns allow up to 3 before bonuses. You can skip twice per game to save bricks. If you have no legal build, **Pass turn** is free.

### Sites and bonus cards

- **Cooldown.** When you put bricks on a site, the other sites of the same type are unavailable to the next player for one turn. That player can continue your site if it is still unfinished.
- **Reopening.** When a site is finished, it closes until all players have taken one more turn, including the player who finished it. A skip or pass counts. The site returns with an empty foundation and can be completed again.
- **Double Load.** Once per game, double the current placement limit, using your own supply.
- **Brick Cart.** Once per game, add up to two bricks to your placement limit. The first two bricks you place are free.

Select a bonus card before placing bricks. You can use one card per build, and it is spent when you build. Select **New game** to set the computer difficulty, choose sites, or change the opening limits.
